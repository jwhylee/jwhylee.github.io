description: "A short note on what goes here and what doesn't."
date: 2026-09-06
tags: ["meta"]
lang: "en"
---

Two kinds of things live here.

**Projects** are finished-ish artifacts: what the problem was, what I tried,
what actually came out. Written for someone deciding whether to read my code.

**Notes** are working memory made public. Half-understood ideas, derivations I
had to redo three times, bugs that took a day. Written for me in six months.

The rule I'm giving myself: a note doesn't have to be complete, but it has to be
honest about where it stops.
